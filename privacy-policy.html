Neon Serpent Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Neon Serpent does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The game stores only the local best score in your browser or extension storage so it can be displayed during future runs.

The game does not use analytics, advertising, accounts, tracking, cookies, or remote code. It requests no Chrome permissions.

Questions can be sent through the project issue tracker.
